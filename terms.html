9. The safety screen is not a reporting system

Read this one properly

9ThirtyOne screens written feedback for wording that may need a person's attention. That screen matches words, not meaning. It cannot tell "we discussed hazing prevention" from "I was hazed". A clear screen is never proof that nothing was reported, and a match is never proof that something happened. It is a prompt to read something now — never a finding.

Do not use this app to report a crime, a sexual assault, discrimination, a threat to someone's safety, or anything else your unit requires to be reported through an official channel. Nobody is monitoring submissions in real time. If someone is in danger, contact emergency services. Use your detachment's designated reporting channels for everything else.

10. Anonymity, and the limit on it

On an anonymous form, what you wrote is stored apart from the record that you submitted, and anonymous results are withheld from view until at least three responses exist for that question. Three is the actual number the software enforces, not a description of one.

What that cannot do is change what you wrote. Removing names does not make text unidentifiable. A cadet who writes that they are the only AS400 in their flight has identified themselves whatever the system does. This is true of every feedback system, and it is said here rather than left for you to discover.
